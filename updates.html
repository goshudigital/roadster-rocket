Updates and release notes

Roadster Rocket is updated regularly. Here is everything that has changed since launch, newest first.

8 November 2026 · Strategy guides

  • Five new guides: a beginner's guide, a stunts and scoring guide with exact points for every stunt, a track and obstacle guide, a Garage and paints guide and an article on the physics behind the game.
  • Guides and this page are now linked from every page.
  • The "Privacy & cookie settings" link is now in every page footer, so it can be reached on phones too.

19 October 2026 · The Ramp update

A big rework to match the Roadster reveal footage more closely.

  • The Ramp: a concrete curl with red neon edges and a steel frame. The car climbs to vertical, hangs upside down about 67 ft up, then eases down on its thrusters while you balance it, just as in the livestream.
  • Kick flips are now barrel rolls to the left. The roll keeps its momentum, so you have to counter-thrust and balance to land level, and landings are graded Perfect, Clean or Sketchy.
  • New venue: the course was restyled after the demo apron, with a single white centre line, red light bollards, neon light bars, pad markings and drifting smoke.
  • Thrusters: straight blue-white jet columns from all ten thruster ports.
  • Telemetry HUD in the style of the livestream: downforce, roll and pitch readout, a gyroscope, and air, speed and altitude gauges.
  • The suction loops and backflips from the launch version were removed in favour of The Ramp and the new kick flips.

19 October 2026 · Privacy controls

  • Visitors in the EEA and UK can now change or withdraw their consent at any time with the "Privacy & cookie settings" link.
  • The privacy policy was updated to match.

18 October 2026 · Launch

  • The first public version of Roadster Rocket: a 3D browser game built with three.js and WebGL 2.
  • A roadster and obstacle vehicles modelled in Blender, cold-gas thruster flight, flips and suction loops.
  • The Garage, with paint finishes bought with Mach diamonds.
  • Sound effects and music generated live in the browser, touch controls for phones and tablets, and progress saved on your device.

On the list

Ideas we are exploring include new tracks, daily challenges and more paint finishes. If you have a suggestion or found a bug, tell us.
