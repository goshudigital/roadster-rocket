About Roadster Rocket

Roadster Rocket is a free arcade stunt game that runs right in your web browser. You drive a white electric roadster fitted with cold-gas thrusters down a floodlit night test track, and every run is a mix of quick reactions and small decisions: when to fly, when to flip and when to save air.

Why we made it

The October 2026 reveal of a thruster-equipped roadster that drove upside down around a ramp and hopped over a row of parked vehicles was one of the most talked-about car videos of the year. We wanted to know what it would feel like to drive something like that, so we built a game around the ideas from the demo: suction that keeps you glued to the track, a limited tank of compressed air for lift, and the need to land on your wheels.

How it was built

  • 3D models: the roadster, the parked vehicles and the barriers were modelled procedurally in Blender and exported as compact glTF files.
  • Rendering: the game uses three.js and WebGL 2 with bloom lighting, instanced particles for the cold-gas plumes and an automatic quality system that keeps it smooth on phones.
  • Sound: every sound, from the electric motor whine to the synthwave soundtrack, is generated live in the browser with the Web Audio API. There are no audio files to download.
  • No sign-up: your best score, diamonds and unlocked paints are saved in your own browser.

What's next

We are working on new tracks, daily challenges and more paint finishes. If you have an idea or found a bug, get in touch.
