Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit Roadster Rocket (the "site") and play the game, and how it is used. The site is operated by Goshu Digital.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, collected diamonds, unlocked paints and sound settings in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising

The site shows ads served by Google AdSense to keep the game free. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other websites, to limit how often you see an ad and to measure ad performance.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. Learn more at How Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message, provided through Google's consent management platform, before personalised ads are used.
  • For details on how Google processes data, see Google's privacy policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel processes technical data such as IP addresses and browser information in server logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Fonts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.

Children

The site is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We do not run user accounts and do not hold personal profiles about players. If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise a data protection right, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
