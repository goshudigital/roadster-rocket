Track and obstacle guide

Updated 8 November 2026 · 6 min read

The Roadster Rocket course is an endless, floodlit test apron, built on the fly from short sections picked at random as you drive. New kinds of section unlock the further you get, while the car speeds up from about 100 mph to 250 mph. Here is everything you can meet out there and how to deal with it.

The layout

The course has three lanes, left, centre and right, about six metres apart. Neon light walls, painted pad markings and drifting smoke line the apron. They are scenery only, so you cannot hit them. Signs on tall posts warn you of what is coming: "THRUST ↑" before obstacles you must fly over, and "THE RAMP →" before The Ramp.

The same kind of section never appears twice in a row. The gaps between sections shrink as you progress, from roughly 80–100 metres early on to roughly 55–75 metres at full difficulty. At 230 mph that is well under a second between one obstacle and the next.

Gusts that push the car around in the air start gentle and grow steadily until 6 km, where they are more than twice as strong as at the start. Barrier slaloms and parked weaves also turn up more often the further you go.

For your first three runs the course opens with a fixed warm-up: a line of diamonds, a lineup jump, a diamond snake, a barrier wall and a second lineup jump. The beginner's guide walks through it.

Section by section

Diamond line and diamond snake

The gentlest sections. A line puts eight diamonds in a single lane; a snake weaves twelve diamonds across all three lanes. Steer early and smoothly, because the car changes lanes quickly but not instantly.

Barrier slalom

Three rows of low barriers, each blocking two lanes and leaving one open. The open lane shifts by one lane from row to row and is marked by three diamonds. You can steer through the gaps or hop a row with a short tap of THRUST if you are caught in the wrong lane.

Barrier wall

A wall of barriers right across the course, from 720 m on. "THRUST ↑" signs on both sides warn you about 85 metres ahead. A short burst of thrust clears it, and three diamonds float above it at about 8.5 ft for anyone who jumps high enough. From 3 km the walls come in pairs, so keep enough air for two hops.

Lineup jump

The set piece from the reveal. A ramp with red chevrons spans the whole course, a red light strip glows on the floor and then come rows of parked vehicles lined up across the track: four robotaxis abreast, three sedans, three pickups or a mixed row, and always one semi truck parked crosswise. There are three rows at first, four from 1.5 km and five from 4.5 km. An arc of up to seven diamonds traces the natural flight path, so collecting them means your jump is on line. This is the best place to kick-flip.

Lineup without a ramp

From 2.4 km a single row of robotaxis or sedans can appear with no ramp in front of it, announced by a "THRUST ↑" sign. You have to lift off and clear it on thrust alone. Three diamonds hover over the middle of the row at about 10 ft.

Parked weave

From 240 m on. Five rows of vehicles parked lengthwise in the lanes. Usually each row has a single vehicle, and the safe lane is marked by a diamond that drifts from row to row. From 1.5 km some of the vehicles are semis, and from 2.7 km about a third of the rows block both other lanes, so following the diamonds becomes the only safe path. Weaves are the best place for near-miss bonuses.

Lane ramp

A narrow ramp in one lane with an arc of up to eight diamonds above it. From 1.8 km, barriers block the other two lanes shortly afterwards, so either take the ramp or be ready to hop.

Roof run

From 480 m on. A narrow ramp in the centre lane launches you towards two semi trucks parked nose to tail. Land on the first roof and drive across both for a roof-run bonus on each and ten diamonds along the top.

Air-diamond arc

An air canister floats at about 10 ft, followed by an arc of ten diamonds that climbs to about 30 ft and comes back down. You need the thrusters to collect them all, and grabbing the canister first pays for the climb.

The Ramp

The giant concrete curl. One stands at the start of every run, and another can appear once you have driven 1.1 km since the last. A "THE RAMP →" sign marks it about 120 metres out, six diamonds lead into the right lane and five more ride up its face. Approach in the right lane and on your wheels, or steer clear: flying into the tower ends the run. The stunts guide covers the ride and the descent step by step.

Air canisters

Blue-banded capsules that add 220 litres to your tank. Whenever you have gone about 300 metres without one, a canister appears in a random lane, hovering about 4 ft off the ground, just before the next section.

The vehicles

  • Semi truck: the tallest and longest vehicle on the course. Its flat roof is drivable and pays a roof-run bonus.
  • Pickup, sedan and robotaxi: lower vehicles you can clear with a smaller jump or land on. Sedans come in six colours.

Every vehicle is parked and never moves. Lineups park them across the course and weaves park them along the lanes. Hitting any of them from the side or the front ends the run, but their roofs are solid ground.
