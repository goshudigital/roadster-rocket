Beginner's guide to Roadster Rocket

Updated 8 November 2026 · 6 min read

Roadster Rocket puts you in a white electric roadster fitted with cold-gas thrusters, racing down an endless floodlit test track at night. The car drives forward on its own. Your job is to steer, decide when to fly, flip it in the air and, above all, land it level. This guide covers everything you need for your first ten runs.

Your first run

Press PLAY, or Enter or Space on a keyboard. After a 3-2-1 countdown, launch control fires the rear thrusters and the car reaches 60 mph in under a second. The exact time pops up on screen and is usually about 0.9 seconds.

Straight ahead in the right lane stands The Ramp, the giant concrete curl from the reveal. Riding it is optional: stay in the right lane and the suction fans carry you up and over, or steer away to skip it. It is worth 1,200 to 2,000 points, and the stunts guide explains how to earn the full 2,000.

For your first three runs the course opens with a fixed warm-up: a straight line of diamonds, a jump over a lineup of parked vehicles, a trail of diamonds weaving across the lanes, a barrier wall to hop and a second lineup jump. After that every section is picked at random. On-screen hints during your first few runs show you when to thrust, how to kick-flip and how to clear a barrier.

The game pauses itself when you switch tabs or the window loses focus, so you never crash while you are away. On a phone, turn the screen sideways and use the fullscreen button in the corner of the game for the widest view. Where the browser allows it, fullscreen also locks the screen to landscape.

Reading the HUD

  • Score, top left. When you link stunts together, a chain multiplier from ×2 up to ×8 appears next to it.
  • Diamonds, top right: the Mach diamonds you have collected this run.
  • Telemetry readout, bottom left. On the ground it shows DOWNFORCE in kilograms. In the air it switches to ROLL in degrees and counts your flips. While you come down off The Ramp it shows PITCH.
  • Gyroscope, next to the readout: a car silhouette that tilts with your car. In the air it changes colour to predict your landing: cyan for Perfect, gold for Clean and red for Sketchy or worse.
  • Gauges, bottom right: AIR in litres, SPEED in mph and ALTITUDE in feet.

Flying basics

Holding THRUST fires the downward jets. On flat ground they lift the car straight off the track; off a ramp they add height and hang time. Lift is strong, but your climb rate is limited and the jets lose power as you approach about 49 ft (15 m), so you cannot keep climbing forever.

The thrusters run on compressed air from a 600-litre tank, shown on the AIR gauge:

  • Thrusting uses 72 litres a second, so a full tank gives about 8 seconds of continuous thrust.
  • The tank refills at 24 litres a second whenever your wheels are on the ground, about 25 seconds from empty to full. It also tops up while The Ramp carries you to the top.
  • Blue-banded air canisters on the track add 220 litres each, roughly 3 seconds of thrust.

If you run dry in mid-air, an AIR EMPTY warning flashes and gravity takes over. A quick tap is enough to hop a low barrier, so save the long burns for lineups of vehicles.

Steering is for the ground. In the air the left and right controls mostly fire the side thrusters that roll the car, and you only drift sideways slowly, so line up with the lane you want before you take off.

Staying level

In the air the car never sits still. Every take-off adds a little roll, gusts nudge the car and get stronger the further you go, and level is a balancing point: once the car starts to tip, it keeps tipping. Use small taps of left and right to keep the gyroscope cyan. Big inputs set off a wobble.

Every landing is graded on how far the car is from level: Perfect within 10°, Clean within 28° and Sketchy within 50°. A Sketchy landing halves your points and bounces the car once. Anything worse and the car rolls over. The stunts guide covers grades in detail.

What ends a run

  • Hitting a barrier, a parked vehicle (semi truck, pickup, sedan or robotaxi) or the side of The Ramp.
  • Landing more than 50° off level ("Rolled over on landing") or upside down ("Landed on the roof").
  • Coming down off The Ramp upside down, or so fast that you hit the ground at more than about 34 mph.

The summary screen shows your distance, top speed, number of stunts and diamonds collected. Your best score and diamonds are saved automatically. Sometimes a REVIVE button appears as well: it lets you continue the same run once, with the track ahead cleared, three seconds of protection and at least 400 litres of air.

Five beginner mistakes

  1. Holding THRUST all the time. You will be empty exactly when a lineup needs a long burn. Tap for barriers and hold only for big gaps.
  2. Starting a kick flip too late. Begin the flip as soon as you leave the ramp so there is time to stop the spin.
  3. Letting go of FLIP at the last moment. The spin keeps going after you release. Let go early and counter the other way.
  4. Flying into The Ramp. If you are in its lane, be on your wheels when you reach the base. Arriving in the air means hitting the tower.
  5. Steering in mid-air. Air steering is weak. Choose your lane on the ground, then use left and right only for balance.
