Garage, paints and Mach diamonds

Updated 8 November 2026 · 4 min read

Mach diamonds are the glowing cyan crystals scattered along the course. Each one adds 25 points to your score and one diamond to your balance, which you spend on paint finishes in the Garage. Here is every paint, what it costs and the quickest ways to afford it.

How diamonds are saved

The diamonds you collect during a run are added to your balance when the run ends. A crash does not cost you anything, because the run-over screen banks every diamond you picked up along the way.

There is one catch. If you choose RESTART or MENU from the pause screen, the run is thrown away, and so are its diamonds and its score. If you have collected a lot, keep playing until the run ends.

Your balance, best score and paints are stored in your browser on this device. There is no account and nothing to sign in to. Clearing this site's data resets them, and private or incognito windows may not keep them at all.

Unlocking everything costs 16,000 diamonds. Paint is purely cosmetic: every finish drives, flies and lands exactly the same.

Using the Garage

Open the Garage from the title screen or from the run-over screen. The car turns slowly on a stand under the lights. Point at a paint, or tab to it with the keyboard, to preview it on the car. Click or tap to buy it if you have enough diamonds; it is equipped straight away. Paints you own are marked OWNED and can be swapped back and forth for free. If you are short, the game tells you exactly how many more diamonds you need.

Tips for collecting more

  1. Ride every Ramp. Eleven diamonds plus 1,200 to 2,000 points make it the richest single section on the course.
  2. Take the roof runs. Land on the first semi and drive across both roofs for up to twelve diamonds and two roof-run bonuses.
  3. Grab the canister first. Air-diamond arcs start with a canister that pays for the climb to the top of the arc.
  4. Follow the arcs over jumps. Jump arcs trace the natural flight path at your current speed, so the diamonds double as a guide to a good take-off.
  5. Stay alive. Diamonds are spread evenly along the course, so a long run collects more than a short one full of risky flips. Bank them by finishing the run rather than restarting from the pause screen.
